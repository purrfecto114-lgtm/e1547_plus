The age confirmation moved onto its own page right before the login step of the onboarding: every way through it, skipping included, now leads through the page, and the login form no longer asks twice. The refresh indicator reports the outcome of the refresh it actually ran - later pulls no longer claimed success without reaching the network. On old Android releases the biometric probe cross-checks more signals, each probe stands on its own, and every answer lands in the log page, so a fingerprint that still reads as missing leaves its reason behind.
