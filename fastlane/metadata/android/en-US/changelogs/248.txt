A note on the release pace: the last few days brought a lot of updates,
and one of them — 22.10.1 — announced a fix that did not hold: the
loading-ring freeze it claimed to stop was still there. Sorry for the
churn, and for calling that one fixed before it was. This build takes
its place under the same number, and the freeze is now fixed for real:
the client's shutdown drains its in-flight requests before the engine
closes, verified against the crash log you sent and guarded by tests.
Updates should settle down from here.

- switching accounts or hosts no longer freezes the app on a loading ring
- playing a video keeps the screen awake instead of letting the device sleep
- the portrait fullscreen video bar spreads over two rows instead of squeezing everything into one
- database exports can include logins (encrypted), browsing history and subscriptions
- every interface string is now translated in all 13 app languages, and a redundant http dependency was dropped
