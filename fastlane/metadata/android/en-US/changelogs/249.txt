A patch build on top of 22.11.0.

- closing the fullscreen player no longer clears the screen-wake flag
  while the page underneath keeps playing the same video
- completing a download no longer crashes debug builds, and disposing
  the download controller no longer trips the documented cronet close
  race
- a sanitized database export can no longer ship a logged-in account
  whose headers were already exportable, and a crashed export or import
  leaves no plaintext credential database behind — the boot sequence
  sweeps the temporary files
- the pools and forum screens carry no hardcoded english anymore, and
  73 translation fixes landed across all thirteen languages (italian
  pool terms, the french vous-forms, korean and vietnamese post wording
  among them)
- avatars, history thumbnails, pool covers and task previews decode at
  their display size, and the fullscreen video bar coalesces its
  buffering updates
