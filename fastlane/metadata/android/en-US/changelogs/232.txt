Videos load when you open one instead of while you browse: an opened video always plays right away, and with the auto-loading setting on, the videos next to it are prefetched too — neighbours that are not videos, or have no file, load nothing. The setting's description now says what it actually does.
Unlocking with the pin no longer waits for the key derivation on every attempt: the session remembers the verified pin, so repeats answer instantly while the stored hash and its cost stay untouched. Removing the pin also forgets the remembered one.
