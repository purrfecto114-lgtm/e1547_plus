The refresh indicator no longer claims success for a refresh it never ran: a pull that landed on a fetch which had just settled awaited that fetch's outcome instead of starting its own round trip, so after the first honest failure later pulls read a success whose data was never refreshed; the refresh now re-runs a verdict that predates itself and reports what its own round trip answered. The fingerprint sensor is found again on Android 9 and older, where a no-op compatibility layer had silently disabled every probe - local_auth's included - and the settings page reported no biometric hardware on devices whose reader held enrolled prints; the app now asks the framework's fingerprint manager directly and pins the compatibility library at the last version that still asks the hardware.
