The pin pad keeps its nerve: keys register the moment the finger touches down anywhere on the cell, presses typed while a verification is still running are buffered and replayed into the fresh entry instead of dropped, the keys and their gaps breathe wider, and the unlock derivation starts on the fourth tap instead of after a fixed beat. The blacklist page carries a filter field now: typing narrows the tiles to the entries containing the query, so finding one entry no longer means scrolling the whole list. The app follows Material 3. On iOS the gallery save no longer lies: webm and every other container the photo library cannot store refuse up front with a message naming the format instead of a success that wrote nothing, and animated GIFs reach the gallery through their own creation request, so they save as animations instead of a still first frame.
