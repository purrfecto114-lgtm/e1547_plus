### Added
- an offline state to post lists, plus a banner showing cached data while offline
- undo snackbars for deleted history entries and blacklist entries
- a receive timeout to api and update requests

### Changed
- database exports no longer contain saved logins
- database imports reject newer files, check for missing tables, strip saved logins and warn about unexpected hosts
- the database is excluded from cloud backups on Android and iOS
- the cookie capture webview no longer navigates off-site
- the posts filter panel applies its changes debounced instead of on every keystroke
