e1547 provides a polished and comfortable browsing experience for your favorite image board, e621 (as well as e926).

<b> Features </b>

- Browse and search posts and pools of posts
- Favorite, comment on, up and downvote, and edit posts
- View the newest, hottest and your favorite posts
- Save Images to your device
- Follow tags, like artists or characters
- Display post info, launch searches from tags
- Access tag wikis by long-pressing
- Extensive local blacklist support, synced with the site
- History of all pages you visited
- Play videos, with double tap skip function
- Supports DText, the e6 markdown language
- Experimental forum support
- Multiple themes and personalization options
<b> About this build </b>

This build is an enhanced fork of e1547, focused on stability on Android 7 and other low memory devices. On top of the official feature set it adds a video quality picker with on-demand loading, password-protected (AES-256-GCM) database exports, an 18+ confirmation before logging in, nine interface languages (English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Russian, German, Spanish, French, Brazilian Portuguese), curated offline tag descriptions in simplified chinese and japanese, and low-memory tuned image caching and video playback.

e1547 is an app by clragon and clynamic — all credit for the original app goes to them and the e1547 community. Updates for this fork are published on its GitHub releases page.
